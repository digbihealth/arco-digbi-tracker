Population Profile
  • Hubs: St. Louis, Chicago, Atlanta, Dallas — Digbi employees are called "associates" at ARCO
  • Younger, mostly professional/white-collar workforce (engineers, project management) — only ~20% (superintendents) skew blue-collar. This is a real deviation from the blue-collar outreach default used for most construction clients.
  • Priority: high gastro claims and a few unusually high-cost claimants, without over-communicating to unaffected associates
  • Only other point solution is Hinge Health (MSK); ARCO does not cover GLP-1s for weight management today
